# AUDIT — explorations v3, files 01-10 (Claude group)

Audited 2026-08-06 against `../audience.md`, `../repurposing-map.md`,
`../theme-explorations/BRIEF.md` (constitution + text budget). Generators saw
only SEED.md, so missing trust components are expected gaps, not sins; the
dishonest mechanics they invented unprompted are the real findings.

## The headline result (cross-cutting)

Naive design converges on the exact grift patterns this audience is trained
to bounce off. Independently, with no shared instructions:

- **7 of 10 invented fake liveness**: animated counters, pulsing LIVE badges,
  scrolling tickers, fake timestamps, sparkline trends, "Vol. II, No. 47".
- **4 of 10 used red/urgency near money**: skull badges, "closes soon" in
  red, "grab 'em before they despawn".
- **3 of 10 fabricated social proof**: invented contact names, "7 of 9 tabs
  left, people actually take these", fake KPI deltas ("▲ 4.1% vs last week").
- **2 of 10 gated with a form before value** (07, 08: name/programme/email
  coupon forms).
- **2 of 10 obscured the price or the product** (09: blurred veiled preview;
  10: "Price upon request").
- **10 of 10 blew the text budget**; none shipped the trust anatomy (fee
  grid, cancel mechanism, Stripe handoff, blemish line, pulse strip,
  closings, eligibility collapse, footer report line).

Every one of these is a named disqualifier in audience.md §6 or a banned
pattern in repurposing-map.md. The audit rules exist because this is what
design defaults to without them.

## Orchestrator note on "theme-fights-audience" verdicts

Auditors recommended abandoning the costume for 08 and 10. Overruled for the
redesign round: the before/after gallery is most instructive when the visual
world survives and only the dishonesty dies. Redesigns keep each theme's
identity (palette, type, texture) and fix the mechanics. Whether a theme is
*worth keeping at all* is the owner's call, made easier by an honest version
of it existing.
