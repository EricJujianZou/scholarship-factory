# MANIFEST — explorations v3 originals

One line per file: `NN-slug — one-sentence design intent`.

01-soft-premium — Airbnb/Linear consumer polish: warm off-white, periwinkle accent, glassy header, chip-filterable listing board, paid tier as "the one paid thing that keeps the list free".
02-bank-calm — Engraved cream/navy "internship register": serif small-caps masthead, hairline-ruled ledger with live filtering, $15 tier as a private-banking "standing arrangement".
03-playful-arcade — Neon arcade cabinet: rarity-tiered collectible listing cards with HP deadline bars, confetti buttons, coin HUD, paid tier as a "Legendary Power-Up" in the item shop.
04-notion-doc — Workspace document: breadcrumbs, property rows, inline database table with filters, toggle FAQs, monochrome with Notion-blue highlight.
05-vaporwave-night — Synthwave night: animated grid horizon, chrome glitch wordmark, scanline overlay, glowing cards, paid tier as a neon "Monday Drop" cassette.
06-campus-poster — Photocopied corkboard flyer: torn paper, marker headings, Courier rows, riso-red spot color, paid tier as interactive tear-off pull-tabs.
07-ios-native — First-party Apple app: condensing large title, segmented filters, grouped inset lists, spring bottom sheet for the $15 tier, auto dark mode.
08-newspaper-classified — Broadsheet classifieds: masthead with dateline, rule-separated columns of tiny ads, paid tier as a boxed display ad with clip-out coupon.
09-map-dashboard — Mission-control telemetry: dot-matrix SVG Canada map with count-scaled city markers filtering the data panel, KPI tiles, paid tier as a Pro upgrade panel.
10-luxury-fashion — "Maison UGMI" couture lookbook: thin Bodoni display, black/white/gold, listings as numbered Looks, $15 tier as "Membre de la Maison". Deliberately wrong-for-audience.
11-terminal-recruiter — A keyboard-first recruiting console turns internship search into a transparent query with dense, live-filtered output.
12-departure-board — Canadian transit wayfinding frames listings as imminent departures and the paid shortlist as an optional recurring ticket.
13-comic-panels — An inked comic issue tells the internship hunt as a panel-by-panel story with searchable job reveals and a Monday bonus chapter.
14-swiss-index — A rigorous red, black, and cream catalog uses oversized numbering and asymmetric indexing to make a large list feel editorially precise.
15-field-guide — A naturalist field guide classifies internships by habitat and discipline while presenting Monday matches as annotated field notes.
16-game-show — A warm 1970s studio set turns browsing into a category reveal and positions the paid shortlist as an honest bonus round.
17-grocery-flyer — A fluorescent grocery flyer organizes roles by aisle, marks salary and closing-date freshness, and keeps saved finds on an in-page receipt.
18-orbital-mission — A bright mid-century mission briefing maps closing dates to launch windows and presents each internship as a flight candidate.
19-corkboard-collage — A tactile dorm-room corkboard lets visitors search, pin, and reorder irregular listing notes around a personal builder message.
20-blueprint-spec — An engineering drawing decomposes the free list and paid service into measured parts, callouts, and expandable technical rows.
