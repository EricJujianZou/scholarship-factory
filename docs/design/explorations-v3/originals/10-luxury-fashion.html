Nº III — The Atelier

La Maison

Maison UGMI is not a boardroom. It is one atelier, one artisan: a single university student in Waterloo who reads job boards the way a tailor reads cloth — and keeps only what holds its shape.

No investors. No sales team. No account creation, no email capture, no paywall standing between you and the 1,427 listings. The house believes access is not a product. Curation is.

Every listing is gathered, checked, and set into the collection by hand. When it closes, it is retired. The list stays honest because one person answers for it.

— Eric

Founder & Sole Artisan, Waterloo
