FAQ

Fair questions

The short, honest versions.

Is the list really free? What’s the catch?

Really free. All 1,427 listings, no account, no email, no paywall. The catch is there isn’t one — the paid Monday 10 is a separate, optional thing that funds the free board.

How are the Monday 10 picked?

By hand, matched to what you tell me — your program, preferred cities, and the kind of roles you want. Ten picks, each with a named contact person, in your inbox every Monday.

Why does a named contact matter?

Because “Apply” buttons go into a pile of hundreds. A short, polite note to a real person — a recruiter or team lead — is how a lot of interns actually get noticed.

Can I cancel the Monday 10?

Anytime, in two clicks. Payment runs through Stripe — I never see your card details — and cancelling doesn’t touch your access to the free list, because the free list never needed an account anyway.

Who runs UGMI?

One university student at Waterloo. You can find me on Instagram at @sleppyeric — say hi, tell me what’s broken, or tell me which company I should add next.
