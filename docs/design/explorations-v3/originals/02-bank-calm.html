Section III

Particulars

Who keeps this

UGMI is built and run by one university student at Waterloo. There is no company, no growth team, and no investor asking for your data.

What is free

The entire register — all 1,427 listings — is free to search and always will be. No account, no email address, no paywall.

What is paid

One thing only: the Monday List, at $15 per month. It exists so the register can stay free, and it is priced like a sandwich, not a service tier.

Where to find me

Questions, corrections, or a listing I have missed: Instagram, @sleppyeric. I read everything, usually the same day.
