Questions people actually ask

Tap to expand.

Why is the list free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. These listings are public information — charging you to see them would be a scummy move. The Monday 10 subscription covers hosting and my time, and the list stays free either way.

What exactly do I get for $15/month?

Every Monday: ten internships chosen for your specific situation — program, year, city, work-permit status if it matters. Each one comes with a named contact person (a recruiter or engineer at the company), so you can reach a human instead of a portal.

It's the difference between a job board and a friend who reads job boards for you.

Who runs this?

One university student at Waterloo. No company, no team, no VC. I built it because I was tired of internship lists that wanted my email before showing me anything. I'm @sleppyeric on Instagram if you want to say hi or yell at me about a broken link.

How fresh is the data?

The database updates daily. Closed listings get removed, new ones get added, and the "closes" column is checked against the actual posting — not scraped once and left to rot.

Is payment safe? Can I cancel?

Payment goes through Stripe — I never see your card number. Cancel anytime from the Stripe receipt email; it takes one click and works immediately.
