What you are paying for

One Waterloo student reviews the open board and assembles a relevant shortlist. You are paying for saved search time and careful curation.

What is still free

Every searchable listing, every closing date, and every employer application link. No account, email, or payment is required to browse.

What the plan needs

The Monday plan needs your preferences and one delivery email. That information is used to send the shortlist, not to gate the board.

What is not promised

No guaranteed interviews, referrals, or exclusive access. Contacts are publicly available recruiting or team contacts, verified when the list is prepared.
