FAQ

> is the list actually free?
Yes. All 1,427 listings are open to anyone. No account, no email wall, no "unlock 3 more results" nonsense. The Monday Drop is the only paid thing, and the list doesn't get worse if you skip it.
> what exactly do i get for $15/month?
Every Monday: 10 internships, hand-picked to match your program, skills, and location — each with a named contact person so you're not applying into the void. Payment goes through Stripe; cancel any time.
> who picks the 10?
A human — the same Waterloo student who built the site. Not an algorithm, not a keyword scraper. That's why it's ten, not ten thousand.
> is it only canadian internships?
Mostly Canadian, with some remote-friendly US roles (like Stripe above). If it's a real, open internship a student can apply to, it belongs on the list.
