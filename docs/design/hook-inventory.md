# UGMI Hook Inventory (audience-hook-research phase 2)

Researched 2026-08-06. Ranked by evidence strength × funnel relevance.
Funnel stages: (1) IG reel attention, (2) DM conversation, (3) free site first
visit, (4) email/return visits, (5) paid conversion, (6) weekly retention.

Status: **awaiting owner veto** before phase-3 teardowns.

---

## 1. The GitHub internship lists (SimplifyJobs/Pitt-CSC, vanshb03, speedyapply) — TRUST / direct product analog
- **Evidence:** verified via GitHub API 2026-08-06: `SimplifyJobs/Summer2027-Internships` = 45,983 stars, 3,219 forks, updated same-day. `vanshb03/Summer2027-Internships` = 8,634 stars; `speedyapply/2027-SWE-College-Jobs` = 8,674 stars, same-day updates. README markdown tables that tens of thousands of CS students check like a news feed. Literally our product category, proven at scale, for free.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Fresh-data ritual:** an "Age" column ("0d", "1d") + multiple commits per day makes staleness visible and freshness a reason to return daily. Applying on day 0 is believed to matter, so recency = urgency.
  2. **Brutal table minimalism = credibility:** company / role / location / apply-link / age. No editorial, no SEO filler. The absence of marketing *is* the trust signal.
  3. **Community-maintained proof:** visible contributions/issues ("New Internship" PRs) signal the list is alive and peer-vetted.
- **Funnel stages:** 3 (free site must beat this reference experience), 4 (daily/weekly check ritual), 6 (weekly list = productized version of this ritual).

## 2. TikTok / IG Reels short-form feed — ATTENTION / our stage-1 medium
- **Evidence:** college students spend ~180 min/day on short-form video, roughly double the general population (~80 min); ~90% of Gen Z watch short video; 45–53 min sessions per sitting. Our reels compete inside this loop, not against other career products.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **First-2-seconds pattern interrupt:** the feed is variable-reward; a reel earns its slot in the opening beat (text hook, mid-action start) or is swiped away.
  2. **Comment section as social proof engine:** "comment X and I'll DM you" mechanics turn passive viewers into DM leads — exactly our stage 1→2 bridge.
  3. **Native-format camouflage:** the highest-performing content marketing reads as peer content (screen recordings, "I found 47 internships that pay $40/hr"), not ads.
- **Funnel stages:** 1, 2.

## 3. Simplify (Copilot autofill + tracker) — TRUST
- **Evidence:** 1M+ users on Chrome Web Store; explicitly early-career positioned; sponsors/maintains the #1 GitHub list above, so it's grafted onto this audience's core ritual. Default recommendation in r/csMajors-adjacent content.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Effort collapse:** one-click autofill across 100+ ATSs turns a 20-min application into seconds — removes the single biggest friction. Our analog: "we do the searching so you don't."
  2. **Tracker as system of record:** once application history lives there, switching cost compounds weekly (retention via accumulated state).
  3. **Free-tool wedge → distribution:** free utility rides the GitHub list's traffic, then upsells. Free searchable DB → paid weekly list is the same shape.
- **Funnel stages:** 3, 5, 6.

## 4. Duolingo — ATTENTION / retention-mechanics gold standard
- **Evidence:** 36% YoY DAU growth in 2025, 10x DAU since 2019; PMs credit streaks as the single most important DAU lever; users with a 7+ day streak are 2.3x more likely to engage daily; Gen-Z-native brand voice.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Streak + streak-freeze loss aversion:** consecutive-week engagement counter ("4 weeks of applications sent") where breaking it hurts; a freeze item forgives one miss so users don't rage-quit after a lapse.
  2. **Behavior-triggered push/email with personality:** guilt-trip notifications from a character outperform neutral reminders; our weekly email can be a voice, not a digest.
  3. **Visible daily quest / tiny commitment:** one small clearly-bounded action per session ("apply to 2 of this week's 10").
- **Funnel stages:** 4, 6 (our hardest stage).

## 5. levels.fyi — TRUST via specificity
- **Evidence:** 3M+ monthly users; default salary reference in student/tech communities; HBS negotiation-class case study; grew from free data side-project to paid services on trust alone.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Data transparency = trust via specificity:** exact numbers ($131,250, not "competitive pay") make it credible; our lists should show pay, deadline, posting age — precision is the brand.
  2. **Contribute-to-consume flywheel:** users submit data because they benefit from data; a lightweight "report a dead link / submit an internship" loop does the same.
  3. **Free reference data → paid service upsell:** monetize the anxiety the free data surfaces.
- **Funnel stages:** 2, 3, 5.

## 6. Wealthsimple — TRUST design reference, Canadian-native
- **Evidence:** 14% of Canadians use it (7x since 2019, Ipsos); over a third of users are 18–24 — its largest segment; the default "first finance app" for our demographic, so our audience's mental model of "young Canadian product I pay money to" was formed here.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Calm minimalism as institutional trust:** generous whitespace, one action per screen, plain-English copy — signals "we're safe with your money" without claiming it.
  2. **Radical pricing simplicity:** one clear number, no tiers/asterisks — maps to "$15/mo, cancel anytime" framing at the Stripe link.
  3. **Canadian identity as feature:** CAD, TFSA/RRSP vocabulary — being unmistakably *for Canadians* is a moat vs. US-centric tools (the GitHub lists are US-heavy; that's our wedge).
- **Funnel stages:** 3, 5.

## 7. Discord — ATTENTION/TRUST hybrid
- **Evidence:** ~60% of US college students hold accounts; 18–24s average 117 min/day; ~231M MAU Q2 2025; the internship-list ecosystem runs companion Discords with job-ping channels.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Ping-based real-time alerts:** @-mention when a new posting drops = urgency + belonging in one notification; the strongest existing "fresh internship alert" delivery channel.
  2. **Cohort identity:** "Summer 2027 hunters" shared-struggle channels make a solitary anxious task communal — retention through belonging, not features.
- **Funnel stages:** 2, 4, 6.

## 8. NeetCode — TRUST via curation collapse
- **Evidence:** the de-facto interview-prep path for CS students; "NeetCode 150" is category shorthand (vs. LeetCode's 2,000+ problems); grew from free YouTube explanations to a paid platform.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Curation collapse:** "150, not 2,000" — value = what's *excluded*. Our pitch is identical: "10 internships worth your time this week, not 400 postings."
  2. **Visual progress roadmap:** dependency-tree with completion checkmarks turns an overwhelming grind into a finishable map (progress state = return visits).
- **Funnel stages:** 1 (the "150 not 2,000" framing is a reel hook), 3, 4.

## 9. Wordle / NYT Games — ATTENTION / daily-ritual template
- **Evidence:** ~4M daily / ~10.7M monthly players across 2025; streak tracking sustains hundreds-day streaks; drove NYT's subscription-funnel strategy.
- **Mechanisms to tear down:**
  1. **Deliberate scarcity:** one puzzle per day — a *bounded* ritual, not an infinite feed. Validates a *weekly drop* ("new list every Monday 8am") over an always-on firehose.
  2. **Shareable result artifact:** the spoiler-free emoji grid made every player a distributor; an "Applied 6/10 this week" shareable card could do the same on IG stories.
- **Funnel stages:** 1, 4, 6.

## Cut list
- **Handshake** (25M students, ~1,600 schools) — adoption is institutionally imposed, not chosen; no love, no teardown-worthy hooks. Useful only as the incumbent to position against.
- **LinkedIn** — genuinely adopted (18–24 fastest-growing segment), but the professional-FOMO feed is unreplicable at our scale; treat as a channel, not a mechanism source.
- **WaterlooWorks** — captive monopoly; its one interesting mechanic (fixed application cycles) is covered better by Wordle's scarcity.
- **RippleMatch** — known among students mainly for cold-email spam; anti-trust reference at best.
- **Wellfound** — startup niche, weak evidence among Canadian undergrads.
- **Notion application-tracker templates** — real adoption, but DIY effort our product should *eliminate*; the insight (students crave a tracked pipeline) is captured by Simplify.
- **BeReal & descendants** — MAU collapsed 73M→33M; its synchronized-daily-moment mechanic survives inside Wordle's scarcity pattern.
- **PrizePicks/Underdog & prediction apps** — strong evidence (58% of 18–22s bet, 94% via apps) but stake-based variable reward is ethically off-limits and non-transferable. Keep only the insight that this audience tolerates recurring micro-spend.
- **JomaClass / influencer courses** — no durable post-2023 adoption evidence; the lesson (creator trust → product) is already our IG→DM funnel.
- **LeetCode standalone** — hooks via institutional necessity, not design; folded into NeetCode.

## Cross-cutting note for the teardown phase
The lists converge on four mechanism families: **freshness as urgency** (#1, #7), **curation/effort collapse** (#3, #8), **trust via specificity + calm design** (#5, #6), and **bounded ritual + streak** (#4, #9). Stage 6 (weekly retention) is served almost entirely by families 1 and 4 — that's where UGMI has the least existing design and the teardowns matter most.
