## 2026-08-06 — Parmigiano (friend, weak ICP fit)

**Weighting note:** friend, already has a job secured, less desperate than
the target reader. Good faith, articulate, but not the buyer. Owner's call:
document, don't over-weight.

**Saw:** explorations-v3 gallery on test.ugmi.ca (20 originals, 10 redesigns).

### What he said, condensed

1. **Theme votes: 01 or 18. Everything else "corny as hell."**
2. **"The Monday 10" block looked "hella unbalanced"** (visual layout
   complaint about the paid pitch section).
3. **Value prop misread, the big one.** He read the deal as "10 job
   recommendations per week for $15/month" and concluded it "defeats the
   whole purpose": he can Ctrl-F the free list himself, and most people
   mass-apply anyway. The actual differentiator (recruiter contact + draft
   outreach message to get around ATS) never reached him. Partial credit:
   he did pick up that contacts were included, but not the draft-message
   part, and contacts alone didn't justify the price to him.
4. **Recruiter contacts ARE valuable to him.** Direct quotes: "trying to
   find recruiters is difficult, so it's worth more to be able to contact
   them." His workaround today is plain LinkedIn. But: "idk if it's worth
   the prices you are listing."
5. **Quantity instinct:** 10/week feels low; 20/week "would be more
   enticing." Also "the weekly structure doesn't make sense to me."
6. **Inventory depth concern:** "1000 seems like a lot but it's not that
   much, especially if people want smth specific."

### Signal extraction (no action yet)

- **Strongest signal: the page sells curation, but the product is access.**
  A sympathetic reader walked away pricing "10 recommendations" against
  free Ctrl-F and correctly concluded that's worthless. The contact + draft
  message wedge (the part backed by the referral research) is the thing
  that made him say "worth more," and the page buried it. This matches a
  known risk in repurposing-map.md: the UGMI 10 pitch leads with the
  number, not the named-contact mechanism.
- **He priced the wrong product and still valued the right one.** When the
  real value prop was explained in DM, sentiment flipped from "why would I
  pay" to "I like the idea you're trying to present." The gap is
  conveyance, not concept.
- **Quantity ask (20/week) is probably a symptom, not a request.** More
  recommendations only matters if recommendations are the product. If
  contacts/drafts land as the product, 10 deep ones beat 20 shallow ones.
  Tally whether ICP respondents repeat it before touching the number.
- **Weekly cadence confusion:** first data point against the Monday-drop
  framing. Watch for repeats.
- **"Corny" votes cluster against the costume themes,** consistent with
  the audit's theme-fights-audience verdicts. His two picks: 01 is the
  polished-consumer redesign, 18 is an unaudited original. Note 18 for the
  11-20 audit round.
- **Not signal (yet):** price itself. He's not desperate and not the
  buyer; price sensitivity from actual ICP would count differently.
