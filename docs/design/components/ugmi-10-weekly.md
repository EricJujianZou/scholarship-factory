# Component: The UGMI 10 (weekly drop)

## Intent
Stage 6 (and 5 at renewal) — the retention loop, assembled from Family 4's
synthesis + Family 2's curator receipts. Everything runs today with zero new
infrastructure: state lives in the sender's ledger; artifacts are plain text.

## Anatomy
The weekly email/DM, fixed skeleton:
1. **Subject:** "Your UGMI 10 — week of Aug 10"
2. **Opening line — the streak + continuity:** "Week 4 of your list, Priya.
   Last week you said you sent 4 — the PitchBook one closes Friday."
   (sender-tracked, verifiable against their own inbox; paused never zeroed)
3. **The contract:** "Pick 2 of these 10 this week."
4. **The 10, as a checklist** — exactly ten, each row:
   "☐ 3. PitchBook — Toronto · $38/hr · closes Fri — DM Sarah Chen.
   *Why this one: they take 1st-years, posted 2d ago, and the recruiter
   replied to a student DM last month.*"
5. **Deadline callback (the ethical urgency):** "Three from this week close
   before next Monday."
6. **The labor receipt:** "This week I read 212 new postings, killed 34 dead
   links. 10 made your list."
7. **Share row (footer, optional):** "UGMI wk 4 — applied 6/10" +
   🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩⬜⬜⬜⬜ — copy-paste text, hides company names, IG-story ready.
8. **Monthly variant** adds the stats mirror: "4 weeks in: 40 roles
   delivered, you reported 14 applications, 2 interviews."

Cadence: **lands Monday. Promised by day, not minute** — built Sunday,
schedule-sent. The drop is an SLA; one silent miss costs more than the
ritual earns.

## States
- **Week 1:** no streak line; opens with the /start echo instead: "Your
  filter: CS, class of 2028, GTA or remote. Here's your first 10."
- **Subscriber paused/missed:** "paused at week 6, back at week 7" — never
  reset, never guilt.
- **No reply last week:** skip the continuity clause; never "you didn't
  apply to anything 😢" — the voice transfers, the guilt doesn't.
- **Day 40 (week ~6):** the accumulated record IS the renewal argument:
  "you're 8 weeks in — 80 roles have crossed your inbox."

## Copy tone
Eric's actual voice — a person writing, not a digest. One commentary line
per pick max. Numbers unrounded, every one auditable. Exactly 10 rows even
when 11 are good — the count is the brand.

## Tokens / dials
Plain-text-first (DM-compatible); if HTML email later: single column, no
images required, checklist glyphs, tabular numerals. No tracking pixels —
this audience checks.

## Anti-pattern (named, never ship)
**The prop streak**: any number the sender's ledger doesn't actually contain
— an invented week count, padded labor receipt, or outcome claim ("subscribers
hear back 25% more"). They will renew for a record and churn instantly on a
prop.

## Marketing reuse
The share row is the viral artifact (in-group-legible IG-story ad). The
labor receipt is a weekly reel ("I read 212 postings this week — 9 were
ghost jobs"). The monthly stats line, with permission, is the only
testimonial format we're allowed.
