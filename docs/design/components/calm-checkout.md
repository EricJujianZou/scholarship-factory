# Component: Calm checkout

## Intent
Stage 5. The Wealthsimple signature inverted onto our Stripe handoff:
**maximum user vigilance gets minimum visual pressure.** Combines W1
(institutional calm), W2 (zero-stated pricing), W3 (borrowed trust +
blemish), levels.fyi L2 (decomposed offer), and the NeetCode free-core
firewall.

## Anatomy
The payment section is the calmest block on the page. Top to bottom:
1. **The offer, decomposed:** "$15/mo CAD = 4 Monday lists · ~10 matches
   each · your filters applied · a named contact per pick"
2. **The fee grid** (the zeros said out loud):
   - "$15/mo CAD — that's everything"
   - "Sign-up fee: $0"
   - "Cancellation: anytime, from the Stripe receipt email — no DM needed"
   - "Your card: handled by Stripe — we never see it"
3. **The firewall line:** "The full database stays free either way. $15 is
   for me doing your reading."
4. **The handoff notice:** "You'll be taken to Stripe to pay." (an
   unannounced domain change is a scam signal)
5. **One button**, single accent color, label naming the outcome:
   "Get my first list" — not "Subscribe", not "Submit".
6. **The blemish line**, small, under the button, first person: "I'm one
   Waterloo student, not a company. Checkout runs on Stripe; your card
   details go to Stripe, never to me."

## States
- **Default:** as above. Nothing animated within a viewport of this block.
- **Day 40 / repeat visitor:** identical — this block never gains urgency
  banners, "spots left", or seasonal pressure.

## Copy tone
Plain weight, declarative, first person for the blemish. Price appears here
AND earlier on the page — the button is never the first place a price
appears (Baymard). The DM price-reveal uses the fee grid verbatim,
word-for-word — cross-surface consistency is itself a trust signal.

## Tokens / dials
design-core read: Persuade × B2C × HIGH trust floor → trust-floor override
applies: motion ≈ 0 here, contrast AA+ on every string, generous padding
(the whitespace is the signal). Single accent on the one button; no red
anywhere in this block; "CAD" set in the same weight as the number.
If the W1 direction is adopted site-wide: bone background, near-black text,
serif for the blemish paragraph.

## Anti-pattern (named, never ship)
**The loud checkout**: countdowns, "3 spots left", testimonials adjacent to
the button, exclamation marks, or hiding the renewal price. Any one of these
converts an undecided student into a hostile one — they've seen this movie.

## Marketing reuse
The fee grid is the DM closing message. The blemish line is a reel script
("I'm one guy with a scraper — here's exactly what $15 buys").
